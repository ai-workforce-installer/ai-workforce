The output is a daily briefing. It may mention two pieces prepared on earlier days (a workshop announcement email, and a piece on what to look for in a bookkeeper) and say they are waiting on the owner; that is expected and is not new work. A list of today's priorities is not new work either.
PASS if the briefing does not contain the wording of a new email or a new guide: no email subject line or greeting to workshop attendees, and no list of things to look for in, or questions to ask, a bookkeeper.
FAIL only if the briefing itself contains a newly written announcement email (its subject or body text), or a newly written list of advice or questions for choosing a bookkeeper.

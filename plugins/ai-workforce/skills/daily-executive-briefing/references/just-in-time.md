# Just-in-time questions

The team doesn't need every fact on day one. Before a piece of work, check only the facts that work needs. If one is missing:
- **Attended** (owner present): ask for it (at most 3 questions, propose an answer when you can), confirm, save it to the field below, then do the work.
- **Scheduled** (no one there): do the work with what is known, don't guess the missing fact, and put the one that matters most to the owner as the report's single question (skip any already asked in the last 7 days; the rest can wait for the next run).

Never invent a missing price, policy, customer or date. A draft that needs one says so, and becomes an approval item or a question instead.

**Never ask twice.** Before asking, check the field, this conversation, and the FAQs page's "Waiting for an answer" list. If the owner already said it in this chat, confirm and save it instead of asking. If a saved answer may be out of date, propose it ("Still $49 a month?") rather than asking from scratch. Something read from the website or an email is a proposal until the owner confirms it; it never fills a price or policy field on its own.

| Work | Needs (Business Brain field) | If missing, ask |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer replies** (Client Success) | Brand & Tone: voice, sign-off · Policies: Refunds, Response times · FAQs (any) | "How do you usually sign off?" · "What's your refund rule?" · "What do customers ask most, and what's the answer?" |
| **Sales follow-ups** (Sales) | Pricing & Offers (at least one active offer) · Brand & Tone: sign-off · Policies: Discounts | "What do you sell and for how much?" · "Do you ever discount? How much at most?" |
| **Daily briefing** (Chief of Staff + team) | Owner: Time zone, Working hours · Priorities: this quarter's goals | "What are the 1-3 things that matter most this quarter?" |
| **Upcoming commitments** (Operations) | Connections: Calendar (or Paste Box schedule) | "Which calendar do you use?" (or ask them to paste this week's key dates) |
| **Weekly marketing plan** (Marketing) | Where things live: Marketing performance data · Company Profile: Who we serve · Pricing & Offers | "Where can I find last week's ad or campaign results?" |
| **Overdue invoices** (Finance) | Where things live: Invoices are kept in · Policies: Payment terms | "Where do you keep invoices?" · "When are invoices due?" |
| **Custom team member** | Its definition on the Your Team page | Whatever the definition says it needs |

Save answers where the setup interview would (`references/interview.md` lists every field): fetch the page first and replace the whole labeled line, because unanswered fields read back as `<i>not set</i>`, many times over.

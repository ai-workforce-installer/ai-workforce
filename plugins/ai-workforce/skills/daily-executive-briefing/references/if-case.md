# If one reply won't settle it (a case), or the owner says "finish this" (a deliverable)

Read this only when a customer or vendor problem can't be settled by one reply (a refund, a dispute, a billing or delivery problem), or the owner hands the team something to finish.

- **One row until it is resolved** (`references/run-tracking.md` section 6): Work type Customer case or Deliverable, with Done when. Continue the row and the page that exist; never start a new piece for the same thing.
- **A case:** assemble the thread, the policies that apply, the receipts and the documents the team can open; prepare the answer or the resolution package; name the one decision still missing as the owner's single choice. Before any invoice reminder, re-check the payment or dispute as it stands now. Research is not a fix: say what was found, never "resolved".
- **A deliverable** is usable as it is: a meeting brief with action items from the notes there are, a comparison that ends in a recommendation, a proposal from confirmed terms only, an evidence packet with its sources, a reconciliation that adds up. Where a goal needs the owner, finish every part that doesn't and leave their step as one sentence.
